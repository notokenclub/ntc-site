LOG 03 ✦ CHENNAI Jul 2026

The Researchathon

Back in Chennai, we tried something new. Everyone picked a real-world problem, like e-waste or urban parking, and researched it the way a founder would: talking to people, digging through market data and looking at who is already trying to solve it. Then they pitched their ideas and defended them in front of the room. Three of them went on to write full research papers.

The story ✦ why we started

We're a group of people in Chennai who were tired of not having anyone to properly talk about AI and tech with. So we started meeting up every week to talk about what's going on in AI, tech and business, and to meet people who take this stuff as seriously as we do.

Nobody's selling anything, and nobody is too new to join. It's just good conversations, usually over chai. We started in Chennai, and now we've got people in Coimbatore too.

What we do

01 ✦

Weekly gatherings

We meet in person every week and talk through what's happening in AI, tech and business. Out loud, not in a group chat.

02 ✦

Paper club

We read research papers together and push each other to write our own. Three members already have.

03 ✦

Build nights

Bring whatever you're stuck on, even if it only half works. Show it to the room and someone usually knows how to fix it.

04 ✦

Events

Meetups, workshops and our researchathon. Chennai and Coimbatore so far, hopefully your city next.
